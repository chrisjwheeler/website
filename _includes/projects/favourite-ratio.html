Method

Each ratio is a player in a Bradley–Terry model: the chance that i beats j is p(i)/(p(i)+p(j)). Strengths are fit by Zermelo’s algorithm with a small symmetric prior so undersampled ratios stay finite. After a short warmup, pairs are chosen to maximise Fisher information — the closest matchups — so later trials concentrate on the hard calls. Stop when the ranking looks stable; extra trials mostly refine close calls rather than reshuffling the order.
